Lantern privacy policy

Lantern is a private, personal assistant used only by its owner. It signs in to one Google account, which belongs to the assistant itself.

It uses Google data (mail, calendar, files, documents, sheets, tasks) only to do tasks its owner asks for. It does not sell, share or publish that data, and it does not use it for advertising.

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
